JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · JARVIS is owned and founded by Mr. Tanishk

In short. JARVIS asks for only three Android permissions: internet, network status and microphone. The microphone is used only after you tap the mic button or the Hands-free button, and only while the app is open on screen. JARVIS does not read your contacts, messages, call history, location, photos, camera, files or other apps. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the JARVIS app and web app (“JARVIS”, “we”, “us”) handle information. JARVIS is owned and founded by Mr. Tanishk. By using JARVIS you agree to this policy.

Contact: aryatanishkt@gmail.com (see section 24).

2. What JARVIS can and cannot access

Android permissionWhy JARVIS asks
INTERNETTo reach the AI server, load fonts and open links you ask for.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo show whether you are online or offline.
RECORD_AUDIOTo let you speak your question when you tap the microphone button.

JARVIS does not request or use: contacts, SMS or call logs, location, camera, photos or files, calendar, installed-apps list, advertising ID, or any background microphone listening.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Your recent chat messages (up to the last 60) so your conversation is still there when you reopen the app.
  • Your voice-reply on/off choice.
  • A counter of how many free AI questions you have used.
  • Your plan status on this device (for example, free or founder).

This data stays in the app's local storage on your phone or browser. You can remove it any time (section 18).

4. Information sent to our server

  • When you ask a question that needs the online AI, JARVIS sends the text of your question and your most recent conversation messages (up to 12) to our server, so the AI can reply in context.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address and request time is processed by our hosting and server providers to deliver the service and keep it secure.
  • Simple commands such as time, date or opening a website run on your device and are not sent to the AI.

If the AI server is not connected, nothing is sent and JARVIS tells you so.

5. AI processing

  • Your questions are answered by third-party AI services that our server connects to: Google (Gemini) and OpenAI (ChatGPT). Which one answers a given question can vary, and the other may be used as a backup.
  • Those providers receive the text of your question and the recent messages sent for context. They process it under their own terms and privacy policies, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse prevention. Depending on the provider's plan, some may use submitted content to improve their products. We aim to use plans and settings that limit this, but please do not type anything you want to keep private.
  • AI keys are kept on the server, never inside the app.
  • Please do not type passwords, bank details, Aadhaar or other highly sensitive information into the chat.

6. Voice and microphone

  • The microphone starts only after you tap the mic button, and the button turns red while it is listening. Tap again to stop.
  • Hands-free mode: after you tap the Hands-free button, JARVIS listens, answers aloud and listens again so you can talk without touching the screen. It works only while the app is open and in front, keeps the screen awake, and stops when you say “stop”, tap Hands-free again, stay silent for a few rounds, or leave the app.
  • While the phone's speech recognizer is running, JARVIS briefly mutes the system microphone beep, so you hear “JARVIS is serving you boss” instead. Sound is restored right after.
  • Speech-to-text is performed by the speech service built into your phone or browser (for example, Google's speech service on many Android phones and in Chrome). That service may send audio to its provider under that provider's own policy.
  • JARVIS itself does not record, save or upload your audio. Only the recognised text is used.
  • You can turn microphone access off any time in Android Settings → Apps → JARVIS → Permissions.

7. Spoken replies

Spoken replies use your device's text-to-speech. You can mute them with the speaker button.

8. “Hey JARVIS” and background listening

JARVIS does not listen in the background and has no always-on wake word at the moment. Hands-free mode (section 6) works only while the app is open on screen. If it is added later, it will be optional, will show a clear notification while active, and this policy will be updated first.

9. Photos and images

JARVIS does not currently upload or read photos. If photo questions are added later, JARVIS will ask for your permission each time and this policy will be updated first.

10. Free questions and subscriptions

  • Each device gets 91 free AI questions. After that, a paid plan is needed to keep asking AI questions. Basic commands remain available.
  • Paid plans (Basic, Premium, Pro and Ultimate) are shown inside the app, with durations from 1 month to 5 years. Prices are shown in Indian rupees before you choose.
  • Paid plans are shown inside the app with their price and duration. Paying happens through a payment provider; JARVIS does not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • When payments are enabled, we will keep a record of your plan, its start and expiry dates and a payment reference so we can give you access and handle support or refund requests.
  • Payments are not switched on yet. Do not pay anyone claiming to sell JARVIS plans outside the app.

11. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts is used for the app's fonts, so your IP address is visible to Google when fonts load.
  • Speech service on your device or browser (section 6).
  • Google (Gemini API) and OpenAI (ChatGPT API), which generate AI answers (section 5).
  • Hosting providers that serve the web app and our server (for example Vercel).
  • Websites you ask JARVIS to open (for example YouTube, Google, Gmail, Maps) open in your browser or their app. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Payment provider (when payments are enabled).

12. Storage, cookies and tracking

JARVIS uses your browser's or app's local storage to remember the items in section 3. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not run third-party analytics or ad networks.

13. Advertising

JARVIS does not show ads and does not share your data with advertisers.

14. Sharing of information

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share information only with the service providers needed to run JARVIS (sections 4, 5 and 11), or when the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of users and the service.

15. How long we keep information

  • On your device: until you clear chat, clear app data or uninstall.
  • On our server: JARVIS is built so that the server answers your question and does not keep a permanent copy of your conversation. Short-term technical logs may be kept for security and troubleshooting. If we ever store conversations (for example, with user accounts), we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
  • Subscription records, when enabled, are kept as long as needed for your plan, support and legal or tax requirements.

16. Security

  • The app talks to our server over HTTPS.
  • AI keys and payment secrets are kept on the server, not in the app.
  • No system is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive information out of the chat.

17. Children

JARVIS is meant for people aged 18 and over. People under 18 should use it only with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used JARVIS without permission, contact us and we will help remove the information.

18. Your choices and controls

  • Clear chat: tap the bin icon in the app.
  • Delete everything on your device: Android Settings → Apps → JARVIS → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear site data for the JARVIS site.
  • Microphone: deny or revoke it in Android settings. Typing still works.
  • Voice replies: mute with the speaker icon.

Clearing app data also resets the free-question counter on that device.

19. Your rights

Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to know what data we hold about you, to ask for correction or deletion, to withdraw consent, and to complain about how your data is handled. Contact us (section 24) and we will respond within a reasonable time.

20. Transfers across countries

Our hosting, AI and speech providers may process data on servers in other countries. We choose providers that protect data in line with their published policies.

21. AI answers can be wrong

  • JARVIS is an AI assistant. Its answers may be incomplete or incorrect.
  • Its replies are not medical, legal, financial or emergency advice. Check important information with a qualified person or an official source.
  • In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

22. Acceptable use

Please do not use JARVIS for anything unlawful, harmful or abusive. We may limit or stop access for misuse.

23. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as JARVIS grows (for example, when accounts, payments, photos or background listening are added). The date at the top shows the latest update. Material changes will be shown in the app before they take effect.
